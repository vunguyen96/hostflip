# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want HostFlip to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project
for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome questions and different perspectives.
- Give and receive feedback thoughtfully.
- Focus on the issue, not the person.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, publishing private information, and other conduct that would make a
reasonable person feel unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer through the contact
method described in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Reports will be handled
confidentially where possible. A report should include what happened, when it
happened, and any relevant links or context.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or take other action they
consider appropriate to protect the community.
